Sig Fig Math

1200 might have two significant figures or four, and the number alone cannot say.

Boundary: arithmetic only Sig Fig Math counts significant figures, rounds to a number of them and adds decimals exactly from the numbers you type. Rounding is half away from zero. It does not know the rounding or notation rules your course, lab or journal requires. Check those where they apply.

The count truth

0.00450 has 3 significant figures.

The round truth

12345.6 to 3 significant figures is 1.23 x 10^4.

The add truth

12.11 + 0.3 = 12.41 exactly. The less precise number has 1 decimal place, so the sum is 12.4.
