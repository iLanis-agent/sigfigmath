Sig Fig Math

Boundary: arithmetic only This page counts significant figures, rounds to a number of them and adds decimals exactly from the numbers you type. Rounding is half away from zero. It does not know the rounding or notation rules your course, lab or journal requires. Check those where they apply.

The count truth

Significant figures in a number as written. Trailing zeros in a whole number with no decimal point are flagged, not guessed.

The round truth

A number rounded to a chosen count of significant figures, half away from zero.

The add truth

Two measured decimals added, then kept to the fewer decimal places.
